Common Questions

Collection Hub FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. The collection managers save information in your browser without an account.

Will my collection appear on every device?

No. Use Export Backup and Import Backup to move a collection to another browser or device.

What if I clear browser data?

Your collection may be erased, so export a backup regularly.

Do these sites sell cards?

No. They are collection-management tools and do not process card purchases.

Will there be an app?

App versions are planned for a future project. The browser sites work now without installation.
